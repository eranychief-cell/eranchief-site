THE ARTIST

Eran Yerushalmi (CHIEF)

Eran Yerushalmi, known professionally as CHIEF, is an Israeli fine art photographer based in Tel Aviv. He photographs and edits his work exclusively on iPhone, exploring light, reflection, movement and unexpected encounters. His journey began with photographs of his daughter and grew into a practice spanning the city, the sea and the human figure. His photography has been featured by Channel 13, Ynet, N12, Mako, the Tel Aviv–Yafo Municipality, Walla, Israel Hayom, Time Out and Artsy.

COLLECTOR SERVICE

Before you collect

What is the difference between Perspex and Alucobond?

Perspex adds depth and gloss with a concealed floating mount. Alucobond offers a clean matte finish with fewer reflections.

Which works are available on canvas?

Canvas is available exclusively for works in the Art Edition, in all three listed sizes. Each piece is stretched, ready to hang and made to order.

Does the artwork arrive ready to hang?

Yes. Both finishes include an appropriate hanging solution and clear instructions.

How are shipping and packing calculated?

Shipping costs depend on destination, size and finish and are confirmed before payment. International shipping is charged separately. Import duties and local taxes are not included.

When will my order be ready?

Every artwork is made to order. The production and delivery estimate will appear in your order confirmation.
