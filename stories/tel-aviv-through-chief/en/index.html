CHIEF · TEL AVIV–YAFO · A PHOTOGRAPHIC STORY

The city that saw itself differently

For years, CHIEF — has photographed Tel Aviv not as it looks, but as it feels: a doubled city of sea and sky, asphalt and water, movement and reflection.

This relationship between artist and city did not remain in a private archive. In December 2013, the official Tel Aviv–Yafo Municipality page published photographs by CHIEF. His work later appeared across Tel Aviv, Time Out Tel Aviv and other culture and media channels, often meeting thousands of reactions, likes and shares.

When the street becomes a studio

For CHIEF, a puddle is not an obstacle. It is another layer of the city. After the rain, Rothschild, Dizengoff, the promenade and Habima gain a second horizon. People, dogs and buildings briefly cross into a parallel world. The photographs emerge from direct encounters with reality — never staged, and never stripped of the chance that made them singular.

“I don’t look to document Tel Aviv. I look for the moment when it becomes something else.”
